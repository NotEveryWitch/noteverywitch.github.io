The game

When is the next update, and what's new?
Updates are released roughly once a month. What's new is in the changelog.

The new major version is instantly available to Officer-tier ($10+) supporters, and 2 weeks after that to Crew Member ($5+) supporters. The public always gets the previous major version.
Where are my saves, and how do I move them?
On Windows, saves are stored outside the game folder, in %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Not Every Witch\Course to Venus!, so a new version finds them automatically.

On Android, install the new version over the old one. Don't uninstall the game first: uninstalling deletes its local saves.

To move your progress to another device, press “Download save” in the Save menu, then “Load save file” in the Load menu on the other device. On Windows and Android, this requires v0.6.1 or newer.

In the browser version, saves are stored in the browser and can be lost. In incognito mode, they disappear when your private browsing session ends. Download a copy from time to time.
Where can I report a bug or suggest an idea?
Please report bugs in the #bugs channel on Discord. Include your game version, device, and what happened. Ideas and suggestions are welcome there too.
Thanks!

Relationships

How do I increase the relationship level?
Get to know the girls: ask about their interests, give sincere compliments, help them take a break from work, eat together, and offer a hug when they are comfortable with it.

Gifts also help: Jenny likes esoteric books, Molly collects vinyl records, and Amy loves collectible figures. Trading doesn't count.

All relationship rewards are granted only once, so repeating the same action will not keep raising the level. If a girl is upset or refuses, give her some space and try a different friendly activity later.

The highest level in the current version is 4.
What is Well-being, and how is it different from relationship?
Relationship shows how close a girl is to the Captain, and it never goes down. Well-being shows how she feels right now: sleep, food, shower, fun and mood.

The girls restore Well-being on their own. Missions wear them out, and a worn-out crew can't start the next one, so let them rest between flights.
Why do pink hearts sometimes appear?
The hearts show that a girl is horny: she agrees to some scenes one relationship level earlier. It wears off in a few days.

Scenes and events

Amy never showers. How do I see her shower, towel and changing scenes?
Amy's Hygiene never drops, so she only showers after Jenny predicts that everyone will want a shower.

The towel and changing scenes start when you interrupt her shower.
Show hint Turn on the alarm and shout “Alarm!” at the shower door. Later, visit her cabin while she's changing.
Movie night: which tapes does each girl like?
Buy VHS tapes at the Trade Ring and play one when a girl is watching TV in the Lounge.
  • Jenny: comedy and romance.
  • Molly: comedy and horror. Try horror when you two are alone 😏
  • Amy: comedy and Astral Maiden Chronicles (found on missions, not sold).
Nobody appreciates the Erotic Movie.
How do I unlock the TV scenes?
While a girl is watching TV in the Lounge, choose “Sit closer”, then “Put hand on her knee”. You need relationship level 4 (3 if she's horny), and the less she's wearing, the shyer she is. Works once a day.
How do I turn the alarm on and off?
Knock on Molly's shower door to learn about the alarm. Then click the keyboard on the bridge, choose “Press any key” and “Leave”.

While the alarm is on, shout “Alarm!” at the shower door to make Amy or Molly run out in a towel.

To turn it off, click the keyboard again. It also stops by itself after a while.

How do I complete the ladder event?
Wait until the girl is near the ladder, click on the ladder, and let her go first (for example, “Let Molly go first”).
How do I complete the salt event (Jenny's lunch)?
Wait until Jenny sits down for lunch in the kitchen, join her and choose “Ask to pass the salt”. After that, you will be able to hide the salt and ask Jenny to look for it during her next lunch.
How do I become a patient in the medbay?
There are currently four ways to be admitted to the medbay:
1) Eat Molly's food from the fridge.
2) Climb up fast on the ladder.
3) Increase the temperature using the machine in the cargo bay.
4) During the day, eat the strange substance from the black lunchbox in the kitchen fridge.

Quests

How do I hire a mechanic (the third girl)?
Post a job ad on FindIn (the computer in the Captain's cabin) and try picking perverted options 😳 You can post one ad a day. Then check FindIn every few days for replies.
Show hint Choose “Looking for a cute girl” and put all three perverted options in one ad: “At least a C-cup”, “Can do the splits” and “Single”.
How do I complete “I should probably have lunch with everyone”?
You don't need the whole crew: join any girl eating in the kitchen, then find an excuse to crawl under the table 😏
Show hint Click the fork and choose “Drop”.
What is the ice for?
Sell it to the Indestructible cruise ship. Your first delivery gets you a camera.

On a mission, you may meet the Indestructible throwing a pool party. Answer the call and ask for “Route advice” to get the iceberg's coordinates. Fly there using the Star map on the bridge computer, choose “Mine Ice”, then deliver the ice to the Indestructible.
Where do I get a camera, and where are my photos?
You get it for your first ice delivery (see ice). The camera works automatically: you don't need to use it manually. The Captain takes photos of memorable moments, which you can find on the computer in his cabin, in the “Chicks” folder.
What does the red phone do? What number do I call?
Only one number works. Hint: click the Indestructible cruise ship ad in the main menu and read their website carefully.
Show hint Dial 1207, then press 1. The compensation is… modest.
How do I open the vent, and what's inside?
Once the Captain needs the key, look for it in the black lunchbox in the kitchen fridge, in the evening or at night. If the lunchbox isn't there yet, it's too early to open the vent — keep progressing through the story.

With the key, enter the ducts from different rooms and look into every opening, until something looks back at you 👀
How do I set up the Lounge? (“I'm sick of all this! I need to finally get some rest!”)
Complete the “Work and Play” mission to get the Lounge Assembly Kit. It appears after the story with the missing girl.

Then open “Ship control” on the bridge, select the Lounge and press “Install”. The Lounge will then appear in the ship's location list.
How do I find clues for the investigation board?
The first lead comes from TV: once the Lounge is set up, watch TV there when no one else is watching. Then open the first case on the board and choose “Investigate this theory”.

The other notes on the board are locked in the current version.
Show hint
  • Quarantine: declare it on the bridge.
  • Harpoon: ask Amy “About investigation”.
  • Something suspicious: use the magnifying glass in the kitchen.
  • Motion sensor: ask Amy.
  • Night patrol: after the quarantine, check the vents in the evening or at night.

Star Duel

How do I start playing Star Duel, and who can I play with?
Once Amy joins the crew, cards start turning up around the ship. Ask Amy about them, collect 12, then ask her: “Teach me how to play Star Duel”.

After that, you can play Amy and Jenny from their talk menu, Flower in the cargo bay, and some rivals you meet on missions. Molly always refuses.
How do I get more cards?
One card lies in almost every room, Amy keeps a few in her lunchbox in the kitchen fridge, the Space Pirate gives one after a match, and the rest come in booster packs at the Trade Ring. When the packs disappear from the shop, you have all the booster cards.
